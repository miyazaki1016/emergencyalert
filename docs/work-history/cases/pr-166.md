## 当時の説明・結論（原文。作者の主張と実測を区別）

```text
## Cause
Production `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` ends with `/`, but the iPhone JASPIC login client checks a URL regex that requires `.supabase.co` at the end.

## Fix
Strip trailing slashes before validation and `createClient`. One-line change to `app/jaspic/oauth/login-client.tsx`; no env changes, secrets, database migrations, Gmail OAuth enablement, or real email sending.

## Verification
Check CI and verify iPhone Safari shows the email input after production deployment. Do not merge until reviewed to avoid unnecessary production builds.
```
