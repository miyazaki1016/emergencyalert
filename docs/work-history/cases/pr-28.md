## 当時の説明・結論（原文。作者の主張と実測を区別）

```text
## What
- keep <1 mm/h drizzle silent
- treat three consecutive 5-minute 1–5 mm/h rain frames as actionable when the run starts within 30 minutes
- preserve the existing >=5 mm/h within 30 minutes rule
- do not bridge UNKNOWN gaps
- do not emit approaching-rain notification once rain is already underway

## Why
The first production rain event in Shiohama was detected as RAINING/INFO but never became actionable. This change keeps the product quiet for drizzle while allowing sustained light rain to trigger an advance lifestyle notification.

## Safety
No changes to JMA source interpretation, palette mapping, UNKNOWN handling, Push delivery, Supabase, or watch scheduling.
```
